Bio

I have been involved in web development for money since 2011, and since 2013 full-time in companies. I love my job, but I don't like outsourcing. I consider it ideal to work on a product that I use myself. Logically, the more expertise in the product a developer has, the greater the involvement and the better the quality. I consider design without data to be a waste of time, unless of course we are talking about prototypes and MVP. Sufficient and correct data always suggest obvious changes in the interface. I can't call myself a specialist in analyzing user metrics, but I can spot obvious things.
I became interested in design at the same time as programming, I consider them inseparable from each other, and code design and API are also design.
I believe that if a programmer tries to write completely without bugs, then he is overly cautious and spends too much expensive time on rechecking. A programmer must test his code, but this should not take more time than design and development. Code testing should be automated, but I don't like TDD. During development, I try to cover obviously narrow and critical places, after development - those places where problems arose.
I have recently fallen out of love with conferences and meetups, possibly due to the fact that for a long time, since the very foundation, I was an organizer of the St. Petersburg community spb.frontend(), now in the community I only deal with supporting the site on Gatsby.js. I was the host of the podcast of the spb.frontend() community, now with my girlfriend we are creating our own podcast not about programming "Ponaehali"
In my free time I snowboard or go karting.
